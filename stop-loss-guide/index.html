How the stop loss works

SL recent swing high/low se manually nahi rakha jata. Entry ke waqt volatility ke hisaab se ATR distance set hoti hai, phir profit ki direction mein trailing stop follow karta hai.

01 · Initial protection

Entry par ATR × 2.5

Signal candle ka ATR stop distance banata hai. Long trade mein stop entry se neeche; short mein entry se upar hota hai.

Initial distance = ATR(14) × 2.5minimum: 0.01% of price

4H se chhote chart par auto-scale on ho to ATR lookback 4H horizon ke aas paas adjust hota hai.

02 · Risk-based size

SL distance se quantity nikalti hai

Default risk budget current equity ka 1% hai. Wide stop ka matlab chhoti quantity; tight stop ka matlab badi quantity.

Qty = risk cash ÷ (stop distance × point value)

Isse initial stop tak planned loss lagbhag selected risk budget rehta hai. Gap, slippage aur fees se actual loss alag ho sakta hai.

03 · Trailing protection

Stop sirf favorable direction mein aage badhta hai

Har completed candle ke baad best price update hota hai. Trailing distance default se ATR × 3 hai.

LongStop = max(previous stop, best high − 3 × ATR)
ShortStop = min(previous stop, best low + 3 × ATR)
TimingCompleted candle ka naya stop next candle se apply hota hai.
